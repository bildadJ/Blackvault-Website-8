PHOTO TIPS (a phone is enough)
- Shoot in daylight, early morning or late afternoon. Hold the phone sideways (landscape).
- Wide shot of the field, close-up of a plant or fruit, people working, crates being packed, truck loading.
- Get permission from anyone clearly shown.
- Resize to about 1600px wide and compress (squoosh.app is free) so pages stay fast.
